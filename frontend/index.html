About us

LifeLine connects donors, patients and hospitals. This site is a student project that shows how a blood bank could manage donors, stock and requests safely.

How donation works

  1. Register and add your donor details.
  2. Request a donation date.
  3. Staff check your health on the day. They decide if you can donate.
  4. Your blood is screened before it goes into stock.

Frequently asked questions

Who can donate?

Eligibility is decided by medical staff at the centre, not by this website.

Does this site sell blood?

No. Requests are reviewed by staff and depend on stock and medical need.

Is my data public?

No. Donor details are never shown publicly.
